What we do

LibrAIry develops AI-powered solutions for organisations that need to structure, connect and exploit specialised knowledge.

Knowledge graphs to model concepts, entities and relationships over complex information.

Natural language processing to analyse, structure and enrich specialised textual content.

Semantic search to retrieve information by meaning, context and conceptual similarity.

Conversational AI to enable reliable natural language access to trusted sources.

Who we are

LibrAIry is a spin-off of Universidad Politecnica de Madrid created to transfer advanced research in artificial intelligence, natural language processing and knowledge graphs into real-world digital solutions.

Our work has been supported by the CDTI NEOTEC programme, which promotes technology-based companies with a strong R&D component.
